Tabby 解决的不是“再做一个 TSFM”，而是预训练 recipe 很难复现：真正影响结果的常常是 data mixture、sampling、mask 和 learning-rate schedule，但过去经常只给结论，不给细节。它还把默认只做 zero-shot forecasting 的开源 recipe 扩展到 classification 和 anomaly detection。

核心动作是保留 encoder-only patch Transformer，把创新放在数据和训练。真实数据来自 GIFT-Eval-Pretrain+ 和 BLAST，合成数据来自 KernelSynth 与在线生成的 CauKerV2；训练上用 PCS 产出可复用的 locally converged checkpoints，再用 DQS 监督中间层 quantile 输出。另有冻结 backbone 的 prompt-tuning，可在不改预训参数下做 in-distribution 适配。

145.843M 参数的 backbone 支持最长 8,192 observations。GIFT-Eval 和 TIME 上它的 zero-shot forecasting 与近期 TSFM 持平或更好；Table 2 里，冻结 encoder + Random Forest 在 UCR Archive 上达到 0.7999 mean accuracy，高于 Catch22+ 的 0.7969；Table 3 里，TSB-AD-U 的 VUS-PR 为 0.4282，超过 xLSTMAD 的 0.40。

消融更直接：去掉 DQS，GIFT-Eval 的 MASE/CRPS 从 0.7368/0.5038 变成 0.7476/0.5117；去掉 CauKerV2 变成 0.7536/0.5109；terminal mask 拉到 32 patches 也更差。边界同样清楚：模型是 channel-independent，不显式建模 cross-variable dependencies 或 covariates，且多数控制实验只在 Tabby-Small 上做。

Tabby is not trying to invent yet another TSFM; it is trying to make pretraining reproducible. The paper argues that the real drivers are data mix, sampling, masking, and the learning-rate schedule, yet those are usually the least documented parts of the recipe. It also widens the usual zero-shot-forecasting-only scope to classification and anomaly detection.

The trick is to keep the architecture conventional and spend the novelty budget on the recipe. Tabby uses an encoder-only patch Transformer, pretrains on real corpora (GIFT-Eval-Pretrain+ and BLAST) plus synthetic streams (KernelSynth and CauKerV2), and adds two training devices: PCS, which yields locally converged checkpoints at each stage, and DQS, which supervises intermediate layers with quantile predictions. A frozen-backbone prompt-tuning module is available for in-distribution adaptation.

On forecasting, Tabby is competitive with recent TSFMs on GIFT-Eval and the out-of-distribution TIME benchmark, though the prose does not give a single leaderboard number. The concrete tables are more informative: on UCR, frozen embeddings plus a Random Forest reach 0.7999 mean accuracy versus 0.7969 for Catch22+; on TSB-AD-U, Tabby gets 0.4282 VUS-PR, ahead of xLSTMAD at 0.40.

The ablations are blunt: dropping DQS raises GIFT-Eval MASE/CRPS from 0.7368/0.5038 to 0.7476/0.5117; removing CauKerV2 raises them to 0.7536/0.5109; and stretching terminal masks to 32 patches performs worse. The limits are explicit too: the model is channel-independent, does not model cross-variable dependencies or covariates, and most controls are run on Tabby-Small at a fixed budget.

core idea

Tabby keeps the encoder-only patch Transformer architecture and puts the novelty into the data and training recipe. It trains with masked patch reconstruction, then uses PCS to produce locally converged checkpoints at the end of each stage; DQS attaches a shared quantile decoder to intermediate layers and regularizes their predictions toward the trajectory between the initial and final outputs. 单步看起来都正常，整条 workflow 却已经被一处注入或坏计划带偏；现有防御多盯 prompt、单个动作或片段 trace，因而抓不到跨步骤依赖里才会显现的不一致。Skynet 把问题改写成 workflow-level anomaly detection：不是看某一步像不像坏，而是看整条执行是否偏离 benign 规律。

它先把一次 agentic 执行转成有向 workflow graph，再用语义编码器和结构编码器分别学“做了什么”和“怎么传播”。训练只用 benign workflow，并用角色、provenance、dependency、stability 四类 invariant 造出保真正例和只破坏单一 invariant 的反事实负例；测试时把 $z(\mathcal{G})$ 到最近 benign mode 的距离作为分数，$S(\mathcal{G})=\min_k\|z(\mathcal{G})-c_k\|_2^2$，阈值取 benign 分数的高分位。

作者在 ETO 的 benign 轨迹上训练，在 Agent-SafetyBench（$111$）、AgentErrorBench（$200$）、ATBench（$497$）和 Combined 上测。Skynet 在前两者都做到 $100\%$ recall、$0.89\%$ FPR；在 ATBench 和 Combined 上分别是 $90.03$ F1 / $82.70$ recall 和 $94.07$ F1 / $89.36$ recall。去掉结构分支或反事实视图会把 recall 拉到约 $90.6\%$；把 $K=150$ 个 benign mode 压成单一原型，Combined recall 直接掉到 $65.59\%$。

这说明它抓到的不是某类已知攻击签名，而是 benign workflow 的多模态边界，因此对 zero-day 和 intrinsic failure 有泛化潜力。但它也有边界：对 indirect prompt injection 和 inherent_agent_failures 这类最像 benign 的异常，recall 会明显下降，而且前提是能拿到可信的结构化执行记录。

A single bad step can poison an entire agent workflow, but most defenses still inspect prompts, individual actions, or trace fragments in isolation. They miss the inconsistencies that only appear once delegation, tool use, and data flow are viewed as one dependency-rich execution.

Skynet's move is to turn each execution into a directed workflow graph and learn only from benign runs. It uses separate semantic and structural encoders, then builds workflow-aware positives and counterfactual negatives from invariants over role, provenance, dependency, and stability. At test time it scores a workflow by distance to the nearest benign mode, $S(\mathcal{G})=\min_k\|z(\mathcal{G})-c_k\|_2^2$, with the threshold set from a benign quantile.

The paper trains on benign ETO trajectories and evaluates on Agent-SafetyBench ($111$), AgentErrorBench ($200$), ATBench ($497$), and the Combined setting. At $0.89\%$ FPR it reaches $100\%$ recall on the first two benchmarks, and on ATBench and Combined it gets $90.03$ F1 / $82.70$ recall and $94.07$ F1 / $89.36$ recall. Ablations matter: removing the structural branch or counterfactual views drops recall to about $90.6\%$, and collapsing $K=150$ benign modes to one prototype drives Combined recall down to $65.59\%$.

The point is not a better graph classifier. It is that workflow is the right unit when failure propagates across steps. That buys zero-day coverage for attacks and intrinsic errors, but the method still loses recall on indirect prompt injection and inherent_agent_failures, the cases closest to benign behavior, and it assumes trustworthy structured execution records.

Agentic serving 会吞掉比 chatbot 多得多的 token，KV-cache 既吃容量也吃 decode 带宽。现有 KV eviction 多半拿最近 token 的代表 query 给 cached keys 打分，默认未来注意力和最近注意力差不多；但 agentic generation 里，think、act、tool 等阶段的 query 分布并不一样，这个假设会把后面真要用的 key 低估掉。

关键动作是把 query 按 phase 分开：AGENTKV 为每个 phase 维护一个小 query buffer，再用这些 buffer 的并集去给缓存 key 打分。它还把这个机制放进 persistent multi-turn serving path，跨轮次携带压缩后的 KV state，并在线压缩保留的 KV pages。

作者在两个模型、六个 task domains、三个 KV budgets 上做了评测。相对 R-KV，AGENTKV 的 task score 平均高 5.5 分；相对 Tri-attention，高 5.3 分。放到上游 full-KV SGLang 这条路径上，输出 token 吞吐最高可到 1.80x。

这篇的价值在于它把“未来 query 不像最近 query”这件事具体化成 phase-aware eviction，而不是继续把所有后续 token 当成同一团代表样本。局限也很明显：只看到摘要，没法判断 phase 划分、buffer 大小和在线压缩是否稳健，也看不到不同任务域里收益是否均匀。

Agentic serving burns far more tokens than chatbot workloads, so KV cache capacity and decode-time bandwidth become the bottleneck. Most KV-eviction methods still score cached keys against representative queries from the most recent tokens, which assumes future attention will look like recent attention. That assumption breaks here: agentic generation mixes think, act, tool, and other phases, and the paper argues these phases occupy measurably different query subspaces.

AGENTKV’s move is simple: keep a small query buffer per phase, then score cached keys against the union of those buffers instead of only recent queries. The system also plugs this into a persistent multi-turn serving path that carries compressed KV state across turns and compacts retained KV pages online.

On two models, six task domains, and three KV budgets each, AGENTKV improves task score by 5.5 points on average over R-KV and 5.3 over Tri-attention. Against upstream full-KV SGLang, it raises output-token throughput by up to 1.80x.

The paper’s main value is that it turns a vague intuition about heterogeneous agent phases into a concrete eviction rule. The catch is that the evidence here is abstract-only: we do not see the phase definitions, buffer sizes, ablations, or whether the gains hold uniformly across domains and budgets.

Enterprise agents are stuck between two constraints: they must handle multi-turn tool use, yet they cannot afford to lose general-purpose behavior. Static supervision is also a poor fit for enterprise workflows, which are usually specified as processes rather than as piles of labeled conversations.

The main move is to expand workflow specifications into persona-conditioned multi-turn simulations, then score them with task-resolution rewards grounded in successful tool use and optimize the base model with GRPO. For enterprise domains, the workflow is written in Agent Script; for public tool-use domains, the workflow structure is synthesized directly. The same simulation-to-reward pipeline trains both settings.

The abstract reports directional gains only: Salesforce Koa improves over its open-weight base on public tool-use, agentic-reasoning, and enterprise CRM benchmarks, with the clearest gains on multi-turn tool use. It also beats a strong proprietary baseline, while still trailing the strongest frontier models.

So the paper’s claim is narrower than the title suggests: specification-driven RL looks like a practical way to specialize an open-weight foundation model for enterprise agentic tasks, not a full replacement for frontier systems. 传统 MTS anomaly detection 主要盯预测或重建残差，但有些工业故障先改变量间结构，单点轨迹还在正常范围里，残差就会很弱。现有 graph-based 方法多把图当作 message passing 的工具，真正用于打分的结构证据又常是事后抽出来的。

GSLAD 的关键动作是先学图，再把正常图压到少数原型上。它用共享 Conv1D 编码窗口，用 condition-aware attention 生成每窗图，再把正常图聚成多个 prototype；Phase II 用结构偏移正则图学习，目标是 $\mathcal{L}=\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{pred}}+\lambda\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{graph}}$。测试时把预测残差和最近原型的结构偏移相加。

在 SWaT、WADI、PSM、TEP 上，GSLAD 拿到四个数据集的最佳 PR-AUC，并在 SWaT、PSM、TEP 上拿到最佳 ROC-AUC。消融表明，去掉 structural deviation 伤害最大；去掉 Phase II 或 uncertainty weighting 也会明显掉点。TEP 的 cross-fault 分析里，同类图到参考图的距离平均比最近竞争类低 34.1%，故障 14 的偏移还集中到冷却水子系统。

这说明它不只是又一个 forecaster，而是在把结构变化变成可直接使用的异常信号。代价是证据主要来自四个工业基准和 TEP 的后验分析；原型数选不好会退化，WADI 上 ROC-AUC 也不是最好。

Residual-based detectors miss faults that first perturb inter-variable structure while individual sensors still look normal. Most graph-based variants also use the graph only as message-passing scaffolding, or they mine structural evidence after the predictor is trained.

GSLAD's move is to learn a dense condition-aware graph for each window, cluster normal graphs into a prototype bank, and then regularize graph learning toward the nearest prototype. Phase I trains the encoder, graph learner, and GNN forecaster on prediction; Phase II adds a deviation penalty, with loss $\mathcal{L}=\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{pred}}+\lambda\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{graph}}$. At test time it sums predictive residuals and structural deviation.

On SWaT, WADI, PSM, and TEP, GSLAD posts the best PR-AUC on all four and the best ROC-AUC on SWaT, PSM, and TEP. The ablation table says structural deviation is the main contributor: removing it hurts the most, and removing Phase II or uncertainty weighting also degrades results. On TEP, the cross-fault matrix is diagonally dominant, with within-class distance 34.1% lower than the nearest competing class.

This is useful because it turns structural change into a score, not just a latent object. The catch is that the evidence comes from four industrial benchmarks plus a post hoc TEP diagnosis study; the method is also sensitive to prototype count, and WADI ROC-AUC is not the best.
