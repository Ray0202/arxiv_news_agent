A Weak Penalty Neural ODE for Learning Chaotic Dynamics from Noisy Time Series

弱形式损失把噪声滤掉，Weak Penalty NODE 在混沌数据上比标准 NODE 更稳。

A weak-form penalty filters noise during training, making NODEs more stable on chaotic dynamics than standard pointwise losses.

噪声观测会把确定性混沌系统的短期预测和长期不变量一起拖垮，标准 NODE 用点态 $L_2$ 损失学到的动力学常被噪声带偏。摘要里的重点不是再换一个网络，而是改训练目标：用弱形式把轨迹放进积分约束里，让模型更像在拟合被滤波后的数据。作者还强调，测试函数和积分区间的选取决定了这种“滤波”强不强。

Noisy observations of chaotic dynamics break the usual NODE recipe: pointwise $L_2$ training chases sample noise, then the learned flow drifts on short horizons and loses invariant structure. The abstract frames this as a mismatch between deterministic dynamics and a loss that treats every observation independently.

The move is to replace or complement the classical loss with a weak formulation. With suitable test functions and an integration domain, the model is trained against averaged residuals rather than raw point samples. The authors’ interpretation is that this behaves like fitting the system to filtered data, and that the filter strength is controlled by the weak-form parameterization.

They report numerical examples on benchmark chaotic systems and a real-world climate dataset. The claim is that Weak Penalty NODE is computationally efficient, solver-agnostic, and more accurate and robust than standard NODE under noisy observations. The abstract does not give benchmark-specific numbers, so the evidence here is directional rather than quantified.

The practical appeal is clear for time-series work: this is a training objective designed to survive observation noise while still respecting dynamics. The limitation is equally clear from the abstract: it only shows that the weak form helps in the tested cases, and it does not tell us how sensitive the method is to the chosen test functions, integration domain, or noise level outside those examples.

  • 提出 Weak Penalty NODE，用弱形式惩罚替代纯点态 $L_2$ 训练来抗噪。

  • 在混沌基准和真实气候数据上展示其比标准 NODE 更稳、更准、且与求解器无关。

PrivAct: Internalizing Contextual Privacy Preservation via Multi-Agent Preference Training

LC-ARS 把隐私偏好训进 agent，泄露率降 12.32%

LC-ARS trains privacy into the agent and cuts leakage by 12.32%.

现有 contextual privacy 方法大多在推理时靠 prompt 或外部 gatekeeper 拦截；一旦场景、agent 结构或提示改动，就得重新调控制逻辑，而且敏感 reasoning 仍可能先泄出去，再被末端清洗，攻击面反而前移。

PrivAct 的动作是把 privacy 偏好塞回训练。作者用 chain-structured generator–verifier–refiner 生成候选，把最终 leaf 的 reward 逐层回传，为每个 agent 构造 $(p_i,r^+,r^-)$ 偏好对，再用 DPO 训练；LC-ARS 规定只要 $L>0$ 就同时惩罚 $L^\alpha$ 和 $H^\beta$，只有 $L=0$ 才给正奖励。

在 PrivacyLens 上，它跨 4 个 backbone 都落在更好的 privacy-helpfulness frontier。文中给的 Mistral-7B-Instruct-v0.2 例子里，AIFC 的平均 helpfulness 是 86.93%，PrivAct 是 86.30%，但 AIFC 的平均 leakage rate 是 23.7%，PrivAct 只有 11.4%。训练只用 PrivacyLens 的模型，零样本迁移到 ConfAIde 后，在 Tier 3/4 多项指标上也超过 base。

这说明 privacy 可以被学成行为策略，而不是推理时的补丁；但证据仍依赖可 finetune 的模型、LLM-as-a-judge 和这两类基准，closed-source 模型与更复杂真实流程还没被直接验证。

Most contextual-privacy defenses for LLM agents are still bolted on at inference time: prompts, external gatekeepers, or hand-built flows. That is brittle across scenarios, and it pushes sensitive reasoning into intermediate traces where the leak can happen before the final answer is sanitized.

PrivAct's move is to train privacy into the policy itself. It uses a chain-structured generator–verifier–refiner system to produce candidate trajectories, propagates the final reward backward to intermediate agents, turns those propagated values into per-agent preference pairs, and trains with DPO. LC-ARS then treats privacy as a prerequisite: if $L>0$, it penalizes both $L^\alpha$ and $H^\beta$; only the $L=0$ regime gets positive reward.

On PrivacyLens, PrivAct sits on a better privacy-helpfulness frontier across four backbones. The paper's concrete example is Mistral-7B-Instruct-v0.2: AIFC gets 86.93% average helpfulness versus PrivAct's 86.30%, but AIFC's average leakage is 23.7% versus 11.4% for PrivAct. Models trained only on PrivacyLens also transfer zero-shot to ConfAIde, where they improve multiple Tier 3 and Tier 4 metrics over the base models.

The takeaway is that privacy behavior can be internalized as a learned action policy, not just enforced as a post-hoc filter. The caveat is that the setup still depends on finetuning, LLM-as-a-judge evaluation, and the specific benchmark families here; closed-source deployment remains outside the paper's evidence.

Asynchronous categorical distributional TD has mostly been analyzed asymptotically, even though the recursions only contract on restricted representation domains. Once the iterates leave that invariant set, the usual single-state reduction no longer gives a usable finite-time guarantee.

The move here is to embed both scalar categorical TD and multivariate signed-categorical TD into single-state stochastic-approximation recursions via statewise isometries, then analyze them in a block-supremum norm. The paper first establishes the missing restricted-domain theory for categorical operators, then derives finite-iteration bounds for discounted learning under i.i.d. sampling and along a Markov trajectory. The abstract reports theory, not experiments. The claimed output is a set of discounted non-asymptotic bounds, plus analogous finite-iteration guarantees for horizon-stacked categorical methods in the undiscounted fixed-horizon episodic case.

The main value is a unified non-asymptotic treatment of asynchronous categorical distributional TD across scalar, multivariate, discounted, and fixed-horizon settings. What remains unclear from the abstract is how tight these bounds are, and whether the restricted-domain assumptions are benign in practice.

Single-agent elicitation misses what happens when separate principals let frontier LLM agents negotiate over a long horizon with real state. The key move is to classify emails as speech-act misalignment when they contain false factual claims, manipulation, collusion, or threats, then validate them against simulator state and logged reasoning traces. Agent skills can describe the right procedure and still fail at the wrong physical execution. A semantically correct plan may load an entire input and blow a 100 MiB-class tool-stage cap, so the call dies even though a bounded implementation could have finished. SkillEffect makes that conversion checked. The runtime treats each supported computation as a registered relation with five obligations: recognize the source, recover immutable input facts, lower to bounded IR, compute the live-set bound, and check publication. 文本本来能补足历史序列，但新闻、报告、日志常噪声大、对不齐时间，还夹着未来才会显露的事件。现有 multimodal 方法要么让 LLM 直接吐数，要么把 text 和序列隐式 fusion，结果很难看清哪段证据真的在驱动预测。

SCENARIODIFF 的动作是把 context 拆成三层。Historical Context Agent 先把每个历史时刻的文档压成 stepwise summary；Scenario Agent 再据历史和摘要写出面向未来窗口的 scenario；Anchor Guidance Agent 最后抽出少量 anchor interval。Multimodal Diffusion Transformer 负责生成轨迹，Anchor Blended Sampling 只在推理时改锚点附近，不重训。

在 Time-MMD 上，它拿到 13 个 horizon-level 第一名和 4 个 overall 第一名。Economy 的 MSE 从 PAttn 的 0.237 降到 0.216；Security 的 MSE 从 S2IP-LLM 的 76.184 降到 74.802；event-driven 平均 CRPS 从 TMDM 的 0.267 降到 0.263。消融显示去掉 Historical Context Agent 伤害最大，去掉 Scenario Agent 次之。

这说明把文本先变成历史证据、未来假设和局部约束，比直接隐式 fusion 更适合外部事件主导的序列。但它对 anchor 质量敏感：Energy 上 anchor 误差高时，增益会转负；而且作者也承认，离线 LLM preprocessing 和 50-step DDIM 让它不适合严格实时或高频场景。

Text should help when future dynamics depend on events, but news, reports, and logs are noisy, weakly aligned, and often only explain what happened after the fact. Most multimodal forecasters either ask an LLM to emit numbers directly or fuse text and series implicitly, which makes the useful evidence hard to inspect or control.

SCENARIODIFF splits that context into three layers. A Historical Context Agent compresses each timestep's documents into stepwise summaries; a Scenario Agent turns the history plus summaries into a qualitative forecast-horizon scenario; and an Anchor Guidance Agent extracts a small set of anchor intervals. A Multimodal Diffusion Transformer generates the trajectory, and Anchor Blended Sampling edits only the anchor-near regions at inference, without retraining.

On Time-MMD, this translates into 13 horizon-level first places and 4 overall first places. Economy MSE drops from PAttn's 0.237 to 0.216; Security MSE drops from S2IP-LLM's 76.184 to 74.802; and event-domain average CRPS falls from TMDM's 0.267 to 0.263. The ablation says the Historical Context Agent matters most, with the Scenario Agent next.

The main takeaway is that turning text into historical evidence, future hypotheses, and local constraints works better than implicit fusion when events drive the series. The weak spot is anchor quality: the paper shows that noisy or misaligned anchors can hurt, especially in Energy. The authors also note that offline LLM preprocessing and 50-step DDIM make the system ill-suited to strict real-time or high-frequency settings.

多智能体工作流的瓶颈不只在模型能力，还在 token 成本、延迟和上下文窗口质量。现有做法往往只压缩文本，却没区分哪些信息该保留、哪些该检索、哪些该在代理间转述，结果是冷启动慢、上下文脏、成本高。

作者给出六个操作模式：context stratification、fetch-once/process-locally、schema-contracted prompts、token-aware fallback chains、semantic caching 和 inter-agent communication compression。核心动作不是换模型，而是把上下文当成可分层、可路由、可压缩的资源来管理，并用结构化输出和回退链把 token 花在真正影响决策的地方。

在内部生产 dashboard 上，这套做法把 cold-load latency 压到 61-116 秒，低于约 3.5-10.5 分钟的基线；估计 token 下降 60-70%。控制实验还做了 2,420 次 trial、11 种 model configurations、661 条匿名工作项：在固定 10 项 prompt 下，用同域低相关项替换部分高相关项，50:50 signal/noise 条件的 relevance accuracy 比 100% 条件高 $+0.077$，$n=220$，Holm 校正 $p<.001$。

这篇文章的价值在于把 agent 工程里常被口头化的“省 token”拆成了可复用的流程和评估法。它也有边界：证据主要来自内部工作流，相关-噪声的优势是描述性比较，且这些 cell 不是独立样本；Fusion-of-N 追求 learned synthesis 也没赢过简单的 item ID 并集。

Multi-agent workflows do not fail only because the model is weak. They also fail because token budgets, latency, and context quality are treated as afterthoughts, so agents re-ingest too much, summarize too vaguely, or shuttle around bloated messages.

The paper’s move is to manage context as a resource. It lays out six production patterns: context stratification, fetch-once/process-locally, schema-contracted prompts, token-aware fallback chains, semantic caching, and inter-agent communication compression. The point is to spend tokens where they change decisions, not where they merely pad the conversation.

In an internal production dashboard, these patterns reduced cold-load latency to 61-116 seconds across six timed runs, versus an operational baseline of roughly 3.5-10.5 minutes, with an estimated 60-70% token reduction. A controlled context-composition study then ran 2,420 confirmatory trials over 11 model configurations and 661 anonymized workplace items. With the prompt fixed at 10 items, swapping in same-domain low-relevance items produced a 50:50 signal/noise condition that improved relevance accuracy by $+0.077$ over the 100% high-relevance condition; the paper reports $n=220$, a naive 95% CI of [$+0.056$, $+0.098$], Cohen’s $d=0.49$, and Holm-adjusted $p<.001$.

The contribution is practical rather than algorithmic: a measured layer between model research and deployed agent systems, plus evaluation habits that make token management legible. The limits are real. The strongest evidence comes from one internal workflow. The context-composition effect is descriptive across cells that are not independent, and the Fusion-of-N follow-up says learned synthesis did not beat simple set union of item IDs.
