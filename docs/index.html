RUIC-SFT+DAO-GRPO 把 27B 平均成功率推到 70.8%

RUIC-SFT and DAO-GRPO push a 27B browser agent to 70.8% average success.

浏览器 agent 在短而干净的演示上还能跟住，一到真实网站就会在几十步里偏航：redirect、动态 DOM、复杂控件和跨标签流程都会把成功轨迹打碎，而只做正样本 SFT 基本学不到“出错后怎么救”。作者把缺口拆成三件事：恢复监督缺失、长轨迹里关键分支被平均掉、评测太短也太英文中心。

关键动作是把同一策略放进共享 browser harness，再分两阶段训练。RUIC-SFT 先用通用浏览、复杂 UI 数据和反思式恢复轨迹做课程式混合，先稳住基础操作，再补复杂控件，最后学自我纠错。DAO-GRPO 则在 AgentBay 上做在线 RL，用 PBRS 加密稀疏回报，再按跨轨迹 divergence 把信用压到真正决定分支的步骤上。

结果最硬的是 BrowserBench：350 个中英真实网页任务，平均 37.9 步。27B 版在 WebVoyager / Online-Mind2Web / BrowserBench 上到 80.6% / 66.7% / 65.1%，平均 70.8%，略高于 Qwen3.8-Max 的 70.3%；在 Tau2-Bench、Claw-Eval、BFCL-v4 上的平均分是 73.8。消融里，9B 的 BrowserBench SR 从 39.4% 的 vanilla online GRPO 提到 42.9%。

这说明长程 browser agent 的瓶颈不只是模型大，而是监督、信用分配和评测都得对准真实部署。它的弱点也很明确：在线训练 rollout 只跑到 50 步，奖励和分叉都依赖 LLM 估计器，BrowserBench 仍是单次 Pass@1。也就是说，它证明了这套流水线能把 27B 推上去，但还没证明更低成本、更多重试的部署形态。

Browser agents look fine on short scripted demos, then fall apart on live sites where a task takes dozens of decisions. Redirects, dynamic DOMs, complex widgets, and cross-tab flows create recovery situations that positive-only SFT barely supervises. The paper frames the gap as three coupled failures: weak recovery supervision, branch decisions washed out by long shared prefixes, and evaluation that is too short and too English-centric.

The main move is to put one policy behind a shared browser harness and train it in two stages. RUIC-SFT mixes general browser data with UI-specialized scenes and reflection trajectories in a curriculum: stabilize basic actions first, then hard widgets, then self-correction. DAO-GRPO keeps the same harness during online RL, adds PBRS to densify sparse rewards, and uses divergence-aware step weighting to concentrate credit on the branch point that actually changes the outcome.

The hardest evidence comes from BrowserBench, a bilingual real-web benchmark with 350 tasks averaging 37.9 steps. On WebVoyager / Online-Mind2Web / BrowserBench, Wuying-Browser-Agent-27B reaches 80.6% / 66.7% / 65.1%, averaging 70.8% and edging Qwen3.8-Max at 70.3%. On Tau2-Bench, Claw-Eval, and BFCL-v4 it averages 73.8. The 9B ablation shows the online stage matters: vanilla GRPO gets 39.4% on BrowserBench, while full DAO-GRPO reaches 42.9%.

So the contribution is less “bigger model” than “better deployment plumbing”: supervision, credit assignment, and evaluation all have to match the long-horizon browser regime. The weak points are also clear. Online training truncates rollouts at 50 steps, the reward and divergence signals come from LLM estimators, and BrowserBench still measures single-rollout Pass@1. The paper shows this pipeline is strong, but not yet a full solution for cheap retries or unconstrained deployment budgets.

SOD: Step-wise On-policy Distillation for Small Language Model Agents

SOD 按步降权坏工具调用，0.6B 在 AIME2025 到 26.13%

Step-wise reweighting suppresses bad tool-call drift, lifting a 0.6B agent to 26.13% on AIME 2025.

小模型做 tool-integrated reasoning 时，问题不是不会答，而是一次错 tool call 就把后续 state 污染掉，OPD 这类逐 token 监督会把 teacher 信号也带偏。RL 又只有稀疏的 trajectory-level reward，在小模型上更容易冷启动失败。

关键动作是把 OPD 从整条轨迹一视同仁改成按 step 重加权。每步用 $d_k$ 量化 student-teacher 偏差，再用 $w_k=\min(w_1\prod_{u=1}^{k-1}(d_u+\epsilon)/(d_{u+1}+\epsilon),1+\delta)$ 调节 distillation 强度；偏差大就降权，恢复对齐时再抬回去。最终目标是 $\mathcal{L}=\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{GRPO}}+\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{OPD}}^{\mathrm{step}}$。

在 Qwen3-0.6B/1.7B 上，训练用 3k SFT 轨迹和 30k RL 数据，评测 AIME 2024/2025、GPQA-Diamond、LiveCodeBench。0.6B 平均到 24.22，较 OPD 的 20.04 提升 20.86%；1.7B 到 42.98，较 36.27 提升 18.50%。AIME 2025 上，0.6B 的 average@32 为 26.13%，1.7B 为 41.72%；1.7B 对 4B teacher 的恢复率是 69.8%，OPD 只有 58.9%。

消融说明，统一权重、固定衰减和错误后全遮蔽都不如自适应重加权，去掉 clip 也会更不稳。它的意义是：小模型 agent 的瓶颈不是有没有 teacher，而是 teacher 信号在漂移 state 里还可信不可信。限制也很清楚：作者只在带 python interpreter 的 TIR 和 Qwen3 家族上验证，web/API 场景与其他模型族还没证明。

The failure is not that small agents lack a teacher; it is that one wrong tool call corrupts the state, and OPD keeps trusting the teacher in those corrupted states. RL is no better here: GRPO-style outcome rewards are too sparse for long-horizon tool use, so small models stay in a cold-start regime. The paper argues that in TIR, student-teacher divergence accelerates after errors instead of drifting gradually, so dense supervision can become misleading exactly when the model needs it most.

SOD turns OPD into step-wise reweighting. It scores each reasoning step with $d_k$, a token-level mismatch proxy between student and teacher, then sets $w_k=\min(w_1\prod_{u=1}^{k-1}(d_u+\epsilon)/(d_{u+1}+\epsilon),1+\delta)$ so high-divergence steps get attenuated and recovered steps can regain weight. The final objective is $\mathcal{L}=\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{GRPO}}+\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{OPD}}^{\mathrm{step}}$: sparse trajectory rewards handle exploration, and dense distillation is kept only where the teacher still looks trustworthy.

On Qwen3-0.6B and Qwen3-1.7B, trained on a 3k SFT corpus plus a 30k RL set, SOD is evaluated on AIME 2024/2025, GPQA-Diamond, and LiveCodeBench. The 0.6B student reaches 24.22 average@32 versus 20.04 for OPD, a reported 20.86% relative gain; the 1.7B student reaches 42.98 versus 36.27, a reported 18.50% gain. On AIME 2025, the 0.6B model gets 26.13% and the 1.7B model 41.72%. SOD also raises recovery from the 4B teacher to 69.8%, versus 58.9% for OPD.

The ablations matter more than the headline score: uniform weights, heuristic decay, and hard masking all underperform adaptive reweighting, and removing weight clipping hurts stability. The catch is scope. The paper only tests a python-interpreter TIR setup and the Qwen3 family, so web/API agents and other model families remain unverified.

方法Method

core idea
SOD 先把一条 trajectory 切成 step，再用每步的 token-level mismatch $d_k$ 近似 student-teacher divergence。若 $d_k$ 上升，说明 tool-induced drift 在变重，就把该步的 OPD 权重 $w_k$ 逐步压低；若后面恢复对齐，权重可再抬回去，但封顶为 $1+\delta$。训练时把这个 step-wise OPD 和 GRPO 相加，既保留 dense supervision，又避免把腐坏 state 里的 teacher 信号原样灌进学生。
SOD splits a trajectory into reasoning steps and uses the step-wise token mismatch $d_k$ as a proxy for student-teacher divergence. When $d_k$ rises, indicating stronger tool-induced drift, it progressively downweights OPD on that step; when alignment recovers later, the weight can rise again, but is capped at $1+\delta$. Training adds this step-wise OPD term to GRPO, preserving dense supervision while filtering teacher signals in corrupted states.
architecture
Student: Qwen3-0.6B, Qwen3-1.7B; Teacher: Qwen3-4B, Qwen3-14B; $\mathcal{L}=\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{GRPO}}+\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{OPD}}^{\mathrm{step}}$; $d_k=\frac{1}{|\mathcal{I}_k|}\sum_{t\in\mathcal{I}_k}|\log\pi_\theta(y_t|y_{<t})-\log\pi_{\mathrm{teacher}}(y_t|y_{<t})|$; $w_k=\min(w_1\prod_{u=1}^{k-1}\frac{d_u+\epsilon}{d_{u+1}+\epsilon},1+\delta)$.
data
3k SFT corpus from s1-1k, LeetCode, ReTool; 30k RL dataset from DAPO-Math, Skywork-or1, MegaScience

在线时间序列预测最难的不是拟合当前模式，而是概念反复回到旧状态时，模型要么忘掉旧知识，要么被限制住不能回放历史样本。现有方法多靠参数更新或经验回放，前者易覆盖旧概念，后者在严格内存约束下又很难长期有效。

关键动作是把历史概念压缩成轻量 statistical genes，再维护一个不断演化的 forecaster pool。先用 gene similarity 做 retrieval，找出最近的概念；检测到分布切换时，就生成新的专门预测器；当池子超出内存预算时，再按 elimination policy 删掉过时模型。这样把“识别概念”和“做预测”拆开了，而且不需要存原始样本。

The hard part in online time-series forecasting is not fitting the current regime; it is surviving when the process cycles back to an old one. Standard parameter updates overwrite prior concepts, while replay depends on storing historical samples, which the setting explicitly forbids.

The key move is to compress each concept into lightweight statistical genes and keep a dynamic pool of specialist forecasters. A retrieval step matches the current context to the nearest concept by gene similarity, an evolution step spawns a new forecaster when distribution shift is detected, and an elimination policy prunes obsolete models to stay within memory limits. That separates concept identification from prediction and avoids storing raw history.

From the abstract, the only headline result is that CEP reduces forecasting error by up to 24% over state-of-the-art baselines on real-world datasets. The paper also claims this gain holds without access to historical ground truth, which is exactly the regime where replay-based methods are weakest.

What remains unclear from the abstract is which datasets, metrics, and ablations support that number, so the relative contribution of retrieval, spawning, and pruning is still opaque. For time-series work, the paper is relevant because it targets online forecasting under recurring drift and strict memory budgets rather than a generic offline benchmark.

Text should help when future dynamics depend on events, but news, reports, and logs are noisy, weakly aligned, and often only explain what happened after the fact. Most multimodal forecasters either ask an LLM to emit numbers directly or fuse text and series implicitly, which makes the useful evidence hard to inspect or control.

SCENARIODIFF splits that context into three layers. A Historical Context Agent compresses each timestep's documents into stepwise summaries; a Scenario Agent turns the history plus summaries into a qualitative forecast-horizon scenario; and an Anchor Guidance Agent extracts a small set of anchor intervals. A Multimodal Diffusion Transformer generates the trajectory, and Anchor Blended Sampling edits only the anchor-near regions at inference, without retraining.

On Time-MMD, this translates into 13 horizon-level first places and 4 overall first places. Economy MSE drops from PAttn's 0.237 to 0.216; Security MSE drops from S2IP-LLM's 76.184 to 74.802; and event-domain average CRPS falls from TMDM's 0.267 to 0.263. The ablation says the Historical Context Agent matters most, with the Scenario Agent next.

The main takeaway is that turning text into historical evidence, future hypotheses, and local constraints works better than implicit fusion when events drive the series. The weak spot is anchor quality: the paper shows that noisy or misaligned anchors can hurt, especially in Energy. The authors also note that offline LLM preprocessing and 50-step DDIM make the system ill-suited to strict real-time or high-frequency settings.

现有 irregular time series 预测常把不规则观测压成固定 basis coefficients，但默认时间戳近似均匀，也把 basis 形状锁死在 RBF、Fourier 这类预设形式上。于是同一套表示在密集区和稀疏区会学出不同偏差。

关键动作是把响应估计改成 density-corrected importance sampling：$\tilde{c}_k=\frac{\sum_i x_i\phi_k(t_i)/p(t_i)}{\sum_i\phi_k(t_i)/p(t_i)}$。$p(t)$ 由 KDE 估计，$\phi(t)=\mathrm{softmax}(\mathrm{MLP}_{basis}(t))$ 直接学习；再用 $M_k^{(s)}$ 做 basis mass 融合多尺度，最后由 feature branch 和 basis reconstruction branch 用 $\lambda$ 门控相加。

在 USHCN、Human Activity、Student Life、PhysioNet、MIMIC 上，DNBNet 对 12 个 baselines 的结果是 5 个数据集里 4 个最好、1 个第二：USHCN $0.4013$，Human Activity $0.0551$，Student Life $0.6136$，PhysioNet $0.3089$，MIMIC $0.4289$。作者还报了相对 Warpformer 平均降 $3.18\%$。去掉 $p(t)$、换回 RBF/Fourier、去掉 AvgPool 或 BasDec 都会退化。

这说明 density correction 确实在补不规则采样的系统偏差，可学习 basis 也比预设 basis 更能跨数据集迁移。但它仍依赖 KDE 和若干超参，理论只在有界信号与非负基函数假设下成立；主实验也多是短预测窗，跨变量耦合不是重点。

Most IMTS forecasters still compress asynchronous observations into fixed basis coefficients as if sampling density did not matter. That is fine under near-uniform sampling, but on irregular data it creates two separate failures: the coefficient estimate is biased by where timestamps cluster, and the basis shape itself is frozen if it is hard-coded as RBF or Fourier.

The trick is to estimate basis responses with density correction: $\tilde{c}_k=\frac{\sum_i x_i\phi_k(t_i)/p(t_i)}{\sum_i\phi_k(t_i)/p(t_i)}$. DNBNet estimates $p(t)$ with KDE, learns $\phi(t)=\mathrm{softmax}(\mathrm{MLP}_{basis}(t))$ instead of fixing the basis, then fuses scale-specific coefficients with a basis mass $M_k^{(s)}$ and a learnable gate. Prediction comes from both a feature branch and an explicit basis reconstruction branch mixed by $\lambda$.

On USHCN, Human Activity, Student Life, PhysioNet, and MIMIC, DNBNet is best on four benchmarks and second on PhysioNet. The reported MSEs are $0.4013$, $0.0551$, $0.6136$, $0.3089$, and $0.4289$; the paper also reports an average $3.18\%$ relative MSE reduction over Warpformer. Ablations show that removing $p(t)$, reverting to RBF/Fourier bases, dropping AvgPool, or dropping BasDec all hurts.

What this buys is a cleaner split between sampling bias and temporal representation. The caveat is that the theory assumes bounded signals and nonnegative bases with nontrivial mass, while the empirical case is still five benchmarks, mostly short-horizon, and largely channel-independent. So this is a solid fix for irregular-sampling representation learning, not a full answer to cross-variable dynamics.
