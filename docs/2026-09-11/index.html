现有 deep research benchmark 还停在中短链：MM-BrowseComp 平均只有 3.0 个 checklist item，final-answer accuracy 也常把缺失中间结论的样本算成功，因而看不出 agent 是否真的维持了 research state。Mr.LHDR 针对的正是这条缝：长链、多模态、依赖约束下的证据整合。

关键动作是先画隐藏的 Node-Relation graph，再压成不可约 checklist DAG。每题平均有 12.1 个必要中间结论、10.4 的 mean dependency depth，而且至少一条 non-text evidence 会改变推理状态。评测不只看最终答案，还看 SA、CS 和递归定义的 DACS：若某结论的前驱没对，就不给下游记分。

结果把这个差距钉死了。GPT-5.5 最高只有 43.1% OA，但 SA 只有 34.3%；o3 Deep Research 是 32.4% OA、19.6% SA。把图像拿掉时，同一个 Qwen3-VL-235B 的 DACS 从 34.2% 掉到 21.6%，差 12.6 点。随着 checklist 变长，在样本数足够的区间里，所有代表模型的 SA 都单调下降。

这说明当前瓶颈不是单点检索，而是把文本、图像、地图、PDF 和 video frame 串成一条 dependency-consistent chain。问题也很清楚：DACS 只验证提交答案里说了什么，不证明这些结论真来自检索；而且 102 题的量级还不足以把接近的系统彻底拉开。

Most deep-research benchmarks still stop at medium horizons: MM-BrowseComp averages only 3.0 checklist items per question, and final-answer accuracy can mark a run as successful even when required intermediate conclusions are missing. Mr.LHDR is built to expose that gap: long chains, multimodal evidence, and dependency-constrained evidence integration.

The move is to author each item from a hidden Node-Relation graph and then compress it into an irreducible checklist DAG. Each question averages 12.1 necessary intermediate conclusions and a mean dependency depth of 10.4, and every item must include at least one piece of non-text evidence that changes the reasoning state. Evaluation is not just final-answer scoring; SA, CS, and recursively defined DACS check whether stated conclusions are complete and whether their prerequisites are satisfied.

The numbers make the failure mode obvious. GPT-5.5 tops the leaderboard at 43.1% OA but only 34.3% SA; o3 Deep Research reaches 32.4% OA and 19.6% SA. In the same-model image ablation, Qwen3-VL-235B drops from 34.2% DACS with images to 21.6% without them, a 12.6-point loss. Across checklist-length ranges with enough items, SA falls monotonically as chains get longer.

So the bottleneck is not single-hop retrieval. It is keeping text, images, maps, PDFs, and video frames aligned in one dependency-consistent chain. The caveat is that DACS only scores what the model states, not whether those statements truly came from retrieval; and with only 102 items, close systems are still hard to separate.

零样本 Chronos 不稳，微调降 RMSE 6.5%–18.4%

零样本 time-series foundation model 在 CGM 上并不稳：这项工作用 8 个公开数据集、统一 context 和 horizon 比较后发现，它们没有持续压过 Elastic Net 和 PatchTST。更麻烦的是，CGM 误差对人群和历史长度都敏感，通用预训练一到临床设定就掉速。

关键动作不是换更大的模型，而是把 Chronos 先当 CGM encoder，再让餐食信息学残差修正，并用基于距餐时间的 Weibull gate 调度。Chronos 输出基线 $\hat{y}_0$，图像、macronutrients 和 meal timing/type 只预测增量 $\Delta\hat{y}$，最后得到 $\hat{y}$。

结果上，fine-tuned Chronos-Bolt 在 T1D 的 RMSE 下降 6.5%–18.4%，在 non-diabetes/T2D 下降 8.6%–18.2%；ID 和 OOD 的收益几乎同幅，说明改进主要来自对 CGM 动态的对齐。CGMacros 上，加入 dietary context 让总体 RMSE 降约 3%，餐后 RMSE 降约 15%。

这说明 CGM 里的 foundation model 需要 task-specific adaptation，餐后窗口尤其吃外生信息。但证据也受限于公开 CGM 数据规模、CGMacros 样本较小，以及没系统搜索更强的 multimodal fusion；所以它更像一条可复现的经验线索，而不是免调参方案。

Zero-shot foundation models are not reliable on CGM forecasting: across eight public datasets and a unified protocol, they failed to consistently beat Elastic Net or PatchTST. The harder issue is that CGM behavior shifts across cohorts, horizons, and context lengths, so generic pretraining can miss the glucose-specific dynamics that matter clinically.

The trick is to freeze that generic temporal prior as a CGM encoder, then let meal context predict only a residual correction. Chronos produces a baseline forecast, and a separate branch consumes food images, macronutrients, and meal timing/type features; a Weibull gate weights that residual by time since the last meal, so the correction matters most in the postprandial window.

On the benchmark side, fine-tuned Chronos-Bolt gives the cleanest gains: RMSE drops by 6.5%–18.4% in the T1D cohort and 8.6%–18.2% in the non-diabetes/T2D cohort, with similar ID and OOD improvements. On CGMacros, adding dietary context cuts overall RMSE by about 3% and postprandial RMSE by about 15% versus CGM only.

The useful takeaway is narrow but concrete: for CGM, pretraining helps only after adaptation, and meal context matters most when the target is excursion tracking rather than pointwise error. The catch is that the multimodal study is confined to CGMacros, which is modest in size, and the authors do not exhaustively search richer fusion schemes. So this is a strong empirical warning, not a blanket endorsement of foundation models or multimodality.

方法Method

core idea
先在 8 个公开 CGM 数据集上，用统一协议比较 Chronos-Bolt、Chronos2-Small 和 TimesFM-2.5 的 zero-shot 与 fine-tuning，确认通用预训练是否真能替代 task-specific baselines。多模态部分把 Chronos 固定为 CGM encoder，输出基线预测 $\hat{y}_0$，再由 food image、macronutrients 和 meal timing/type 预测残差 $\Delta\hat{y}$。残差乘上 $f_{\mathrm{Weibull}}(\Delta t)$，其中 $\Delta t$ 是距最近一餐的时间，最后得到 $\hat{y}=\mu_{\mathrm{CGM}}+\sigma_{\mathrm{CGM}}[\hat{y}_0+r(\Delta t)]$。
First, they benchmark Chronos-Bolt, Chronos2-Small, and TimesFM-2.5 on eight public CGM datasets under the same context-length and horizon grid, comparing zero-shot use with fine-tuning. For multimodal forecasting, Chronos is frozen as the CGM encoder and produces a baseline prediction $\hat{y}_0$, while a separate meal branch predicts a residual $\Delta\hat{y}$ from food images, macronutrients, and meal timing/type. That residual is multiplied by a Weibull gate $f_{\mathrm{Weibull}}(\Delta t)$, where $\Delta t$ is time since the last meal, and then added back to the baseline to form the final forecast $\hat{y}=\mu_{\mathrm{CGM}}+\sigma_{\mathrm{CGM}}[\hat{y}_0+r(\Delta t)]$.

问题不是模型不够大，而是它们看不见云：短期 PV/GHI 误差主要来自云致 ramp，纯历史数值序列只能在跌落后补救。已有 LLM time-series 方案虽然省数据，但仍基本是单模态；sky image 提供的前视信息又恰好最缺配对数据。

关键动作是在进入 frozen LLM 前先对齐两种模态，再做 token 级双向调制。轻量 CNN 把 sky frames 编成视觉 token，并按与 time-series patches 相同的时间索引聚合；随后两支 token 互相生成 scale/shift，做类似 FiLM 的 affine modulation，得到 $z^{\mathrm{fuse}}$ 后再走 Time-LLM 式 reprogramming。

在 SIRTA 和 SKIPP’D 上，$H\in\{16,32,64\}$ 的 normalized MSE 都优于 Time-LLM、PatchTST、DLinear 和 SPM。最强结果出现在 SIRTA 的 $H{=}32$，MSE 为 $0.2557$，相对最佳基线降 $25.4\%$；SKIPP’D 的最佳点是 $H{=}64$，MSE 为 $0.2069$，降 $14.5\%$。只用 $5\%$ 到 $10\%$ 训练日时，它多数设置仍领先，而常规深度基线常被 SPM 反超。

这说明 sky image 不是锦上添花，而是在云多、跨度长、数据少时补上了数值序列的盲区。但它也不是处处稳赢：收益主要集中在 cloudy 条件，SKIPP’D 的 $H{=}64$ fusion ablation 里 Concat 还略胜一筹；作者也承认 ground-based camera 只能给出局部、短时线索。

The failure mode is not model size but blindness to clouds. Short-horizon PV and GHI error is dominated by cloud-induced ramps, and pure time-series models only react after the drop has already happened. LLM-based forecasters help with data efficiency, but they still treat the problem as unimodal numerical forecasting, while sky images carry the missing forward-looking signal and are hardest to collect at each site.

SolCloudLLM moves the image stream in before the LLM reprogramming step. A lightweight CNN turns aligned sky frames into visual tokens at the same temporal indices as the time-series patches. Two small networks then generate FiLM-style scale and shift terms in both directions, so image tokens modulate series tokens and series tokens modulate image tokens before the two streams are concatenated, projected to $z^{\mathrm{fuse}}$, and mapped into a frozen GPT-2 backbone.

On SIRTA and SKIPP’D, the model wins on normalized MSE for $H\in\{16,32,64\}$. The strongest full-data gain is on SIRTA at $H{=}32$: $0.2557$ MSE, a $25.4\%$ drop versus the best baseline. On SKIPP’D, the best point is $H{=}64$: $0.2069$ MSE, $14.5\%$ better than the best baseline. In the $5\%$-$10\%$ few-shot setting, SolCloudLLM usually stays ahead while Time-LLM, PatchTST, and DLinear often fall below the physics-based SPM.

The claim that holds up is narrower than the headline suggests: the visual gain is concentrated on cloudy regimes and longer horizons, where numerical history is blind to incoming ramps. The paper also shows a real edge case: in the SKIPP’D fusion ablation, plain concatenation is slightly better at $H{=}64$, and the authors note that ground-based cameras only provide local, short-lived cues. Good fit for solar forecasting; thin evidence beyond that niche.

长时程操控里，问题不只是“要不要记忆”，而是记忆该放在策略里还是放在外部接口里。现有 agentic 系统常把 VLA、planner 和几何工具混在一起，深度信息、校准几何和额外工具会把改进来源搅在一起，难分到底是政策学会了，还是输入更强了。

2AM 的关键动作是把任务记忆压到 multimodal Agent 侧，再让一个仅基于 RGB、episodically stateless 的 Action Model 只负责执行。Agent 把交互历史编成 subtask language，并可附带 2D grasp、place、move hints，用来在不同时间尺度上绑定物理意图；训练时再用 structured hint labels、condition dropout、spatial noise 和 temporal jitter，让 VLA 学会容忍不完美的 Agent 输出。

在 LIBERO-Mem 上，2AM 不用 depth、online geometry 或 planner-based object motion，平均完成率到 $76.3\%$，比最强已报 baseline 的 $14.8\%$ 高 $61.5$ 个点；同时 relaxed success 为 $63.0\%$，strict success 为 $11.8\%$。这组结果支持“任务记忆可以留在 Agent 侧”，也说明动作模型的表现不只取决于学到了什么，还取决于 Agent 能把意图说得多准。

但这篇文稿只给了摘要，能确认的只有这个受限设定和 LIBERO-Mem 上的主结果。它更像是在做归因隔离，而不是证明这种分工对更复杂的真实机器人场景同样稳健；没有正文时，也看不到 hint 形式、噪声强度和 dropout 设计各自贡献了多少。

Long-horizon manipulation is not just a memory problem; it is also a question of where the memory lives. Existing agentic stacks mix VLA policies with planners and geometric tools, so gains from depth, calibration, or extra tooling are hard to separate from genuine policy improvement.

2AM makes a deliberately constrained split: a multimodal Agent holds the task memory, while a single RGB-only, episodically stateless Action Model executes motion.
