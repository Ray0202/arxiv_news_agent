LLM 做 time-series reasoning 还很脆：图像会误导，数字会幻觉，文本 context 也可能压过信号。现有 zero-shot 方法要么把多模态硬串成文本，挤爆上下文；要么在单模型里隐式融合，结果是证据互相干扰，不是互相校正。

TS-Debate 的动作很直接：先用 $q$ 和 $c$ 诱导 domain priors，再把 $\mathbf{x}_{1:T}$ 拆成 text、visual、numerical 三路，由专门 analyst 先独立写出 observations、inferences 和 limits。之后 reviewer 用 $\textsf{Lookup}(\cdot)$ 和轻量 code execution 逐条核验 $\Omega$ 里的 claims，给出 $V_j(\omega)$、$D_j(\omega)$ 和 $\Delta_j$，最后 synthesizer 按 temporal scope 做 calibration，而不是投票。

在 MTBench、TimerBed、TSQA 的 20 个任务上，它在分类和 QA 上都压过强基线。论文给出 MTBench Finance 0.543 vs 0.445，Weather 0.557 vs 0.523；tool-matched 下 classification 0.47 vs 0.39、QA 0.83 vs 0.67、MAE 20.46 vs 23.32。分类 pooled 的显著性是 $p=3.6\times10^{-24}$，QA 是 $p=3.4\times10^{-8}$。

这说明瓶颈不是再堆 modality，而是把证据组织成可验证的 debate。它最强的地方是 global structure、cross-view consistency 和相对量；在 local value reconstruction 上不占优，而且要付出约 $71$ 秒和 $0.033$/sample 的开销。

LLMs are still brittle at time-series reasoning: plots can mislead, numbers can be hallucinated, and text context can override the signal. Existing zero-shot methods either serialize modalities into text and blow up context, or fuse them implicitly so evidence interferes instead of correcting itself.

TS-Debate makes a sharper move. It first elicits domain priors from $q$ and $c$, then splits $\mathbf{x}_{1:T}$ into text, visual, and numerical views and lets specialized analysts write separate observations, inferences, and limits. Reviewers then verify claims with $\textsf{Lookup}(\cdot)$ and lightweight code execution, assign $V_j(\omega)$, $D_j(\omega)$, and $\Delta_j$, and the final synthesizer calibrates by temporal scope instead of voting.

On 20 tasks across MTBench, TimerBed, and TSQA, it beats strong baselines on classification and QA. The paper reports MTBench Finance at 0.543 vs 0.445 and Weather at 0.557 vs 0.523; in the tool-matched setup, classification is 0.47 vs 0.39, QA is 0.83 vs 0.67, and MAE is 20.46 vs 23.32. Pooled classification significance is $p=3.6\times10^{-24}$, and QA is $p=3.4\times10^{-8}$.

The lesson is narrower than the framing: the bottleneck is evidence organization, not more modalities. TS-Debate helps most when the answer depends on global structure, cross-view consistency, or relative quantities; it is weaker on local value reconstruction, and it costs about $71$ seconds and $0.033$ per sample.

多轮 agentic RL 不是单纯“变长”就会变差，而是训练信号先乱了：rollout 常用截断或摘要上下文，训练却拿完整历史重算 ratio，导致 context mismatch；终局 reward 又太稀，单个 turn 的贡献被抹平；并行采样下，短轨迹先更新、长轨迹后优化，还会把旧 policy 的漂移带进来。PPO、GRPO、SeeUPO、TreeGRPO、ARPO 都只碰到一部分。

RTPO 的关键动作是把多轮交互改写成 turn-boundary MDP，再按逆序训练：先处理最后一轮，再往前回推。每个 turn 都从同一个边界状态 $S_k$ fork 出 $G-1$ 个 sibling rollout，在同一上下文 $c_k$ 下估计 turn-level advantage $A^H_{j,k}$，这样 rollout 和 training 的条件一致，credit 也只记到当前 turn。后续 turn 已先更新，因此前面的 turn 可以对着已更新的 continuation policy 学。

实验用 Qwen3-8B，配 Python 和 web-search tools，覆盖 12 个多轮 agentic RL 设定。主表 8 个 benchmark 上，RTPO 的整体 accuracy 是 61.66，较 vanilla 的 36.97 提升 66.78%，也比 GRPO 的 53.71 高 21.50%，比 SeeUPO 的 57.68 高 10.76%。在受控的 credit assignment 对照里，RTPO-CA 在 AMC23、AIME25、MATH500 上拿到最好的 Pass@1/Pass@4 组合；在 GAIA、WebWalkerQA、XBench 上，on-policy 版本的 hit rate 也高于 off-policy 版本。

这篇工作的价值在于，它不是再调一个 reward，而是把训练管线本身拆开重做：先对齐上下文，再把 credit 归到 turn，再用 on-policy continuation 避免长 horizon 漂移。它对长程 tool-use agent 很对症，但证据主要来自 Qwen3-8B 和这些数学/知识 reasoning 基准；在 HLE 上增益几乎为零，说明收益并不均匀。

Multi-turn agentic RL does not fail just because episodes get longer. It fails because the training signal becomes inconsistent: rollouts are often generated under truncated or summarized context, while training recomputes ratios under the full history, so the optimizer no longer matches the data-generating policy. Sparse terminal rewards then smear credit across turns, and asynchronous sampling lets short trajectories update the policy before long ones finish, which imports drift. PPO, GRPO, SeeUPO, TreeGRPO, and ARPO each address only part of this.

RTPO's move is to rewrite a multi-turn interaction as a turn-boundary MDP and train in reverse order. Each turn is optimized from the same boundary state $S_k$, with $G-1$ sibling rollouts forked from that point under the same context $c_k$, so the advantage $A^H_{j,k}$ is estimated at turn level rather than smeared over the whole trajectory. Because later turns are updated first, earlier turns can be trained against an already-updated continuation policy, which is how RTPO suppresses drift.

The paper evaluates Qwen3-8B with Python and web-search tools across 12 multi-turn agentic RL settings. On the main table of eight benchmarks, RTPO reaches 61.66 overall accuracy, versus 36.97 for vanilla, 53.71 for GRPO, and 57.68 for SeeUPO. That is a 66.78% gain over vanilla, 21.50% over GRPO, and 10.76% over SeeUPO. In the controlled credit-assignment study, RTPO-CA gets the best Pass@1/Pass@4 pair on AMC23, AIME25, and MATH500, and ties the best Pass@4 on AIME24. On GAIA, WebWalkerQA, and XBench, the on-policy variant beats the off-policy one in output-hit rate.

The contribution is less a new reward than a repair of the training pipeline: align rollout and training context, localize credit to the current turn, and remove stale downstream continuations. That is a sensible fix for long-horizon tool-using agents. The evidence is still tied to Qwen3-8B and the reported math/knowledge benchmarks, and the gain is not uniform: on HLE the on-policy variant is essentially tied, so the benefit depends on horizon and task structure.

这说明 failure 不是“不会用记忆”，而是不会把有限验证预算花在真正会翻案的链上。但这个结论也有边界：forced-critical policy 依赖实验者知道哪条是关键路径，因而是测风险上限，不是可直接部署的调度器；同一套预算下，位置选择与内容提示的效果也并不稳定。

Provenance can keep an inherited belief reachable, but a model with a limited verification budget still has to choose which links to inspect. The failure mode here is a stale consolidated memory: the constraint is still present, but the source record has been superseded and now withdraws it. If the agent trusts the surface form, it can keep acting on a decision rule that is no longer valid.

The trick is to spend one of the same two verification slots on the critical path instead of on whatever looks settled. The paper studies a six-memory setup with budget $2$, and uses a forced-critical policy to ask a narrow question: how much stale-decision risk can the same budget recover when the experimenter already knows which path matters?

Across $16$ language models, the constraint’s provenance path was inspected in only about one episode in five. Once the constraint had been superseded, stale-consistent decisions reached $77.3\%$, $74.7\%$, and $74.7\%$ in the primary run, a replication, and a held-out domain. Reassigning one slot to the critical path removed most of those errors: gains were $+74.0$, $+72.7$, and $+61.3$ points, $+80.7$ in a prospectively frozen interleaved replication with a repaired non-critical control, and $+62.0$ on a $10$-model, $9$-organisation panel; a corrected rerun of the held-out scenario gave $+73.3$.

The paper’s main contribution is diagnostic rather than prescriptive: it quantifies how much stale-decision risk is hiding behind underused verification budget. The limit is equally clear. The forced-critical policy uses experimenter knowledge of the critical path, so it measures recoverable risk, not a deployable scheduler. The new experiments also suggest that a one-sentence, target-blind rule can redirect allocation in one setting, while a content-free freshness cue does not move behavior much.

长程 LLM agent 的 RL 常把稀疏终点奖励广播到整条轨迹，结果是少数真正决定成败的动作和无关动作一起背锅。现有细粒度 credit 多从 rollout 侧做相似状态、分支、事后回看或过程反馈推断，还是把 verifier 当成一个标量，丢掉了它内部的任务结构。

VICT 的动作是把终点 verifier 先拆成可执行/有证据的 atoms，再把轨迹里的观测、写入和 commit 证据连成 proof graph，只沿 dependency-valid 的 proof edge 重分配 group-relative advantage。若证据不全、依赖不清或 core 搜不到，它就 abstain；训练时只改 advantage tensor，不加 critic、process labels 或 inference-time verifier。

在 ALFWorld 和 WebShop 上，Qwen2.5-1.5B/7B-Instruct 相比 GRPO 分别拿到更高结果：1.5B 的 ALFWorld average success 到 91.0、WebShop strict success 到 81.7；7B 则到 93.7 和 83.6，分别比 GRPO 高 18.2 和 17.5 个点。提升集中在 Look、Cool、Pick2 以及 WebShop 的最终购买正确性，说明它在把奖励往“先找证据、后提交”这类动作上挪。

但这套方法只适用于能被 instrument 的程序化 verifier；黑箱 judge、隐藏状态或主要受探索失败支配的任务都不合适。作者也承认 instrumentation 有工程成本。更重要的是，proof edge 只保证 eligibility，不保证因果必要性，所以它更像一个可审计的 credit 接口，而不是通用的奖励分解答案。

Long-horizon LLM agents are usually trained with sparse terminal rewards that get broadcast to every action, so the steps that actually decide success get mixed together with the ones that merely happen nearby. Recent fine-grained credit methods try to recover signal from the rollout side using repeated states, branches, hindsight, or process feedback, but they still flatten the verifier into a scalar and lose its internal task structure.

VICT’s move is to expose that structure instead. It decomposes a terminal verifier into executable or evidence-backed atoms, then links trajectory evidence to those atoms through a proof graph and only redistributes group-relative advantage along dependency-valid proof edges. If evidence is missing, dependencies are ambiguous, or the dependency-closed core cannot be found, VICT abstains. The training loop changes only the advantage tensor; there is no critic, no process labels, and no inference-time verifier access.

On ALFWorld and WebShop, VICT beats GRPO on both model sizes. With Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct, it reaches 91.0 average success on ALFWorld and 81.7 strict success on WebShop, up 18.2 and 24.9 points over GRPO. With Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct, it reaches 93.7 and 83.6, up 16.1 and 17.5 points. The biggest gains land where the verifier exposes delayed or commit-sensitive facts: Look, Cool, Pick2, and final purchase correctness.

The paper’s real claim is narrower than its framing: this is not a general reward decomposition method, but a verifier interface for tasks whose terminal judge can be instrumented. That makes the method auditable, but also ties it to engineering effort and to verifiers with observable evidence, commit actions, and dependency structure. The authors are explicit that proof edges certify eligibility, not causal necessity, so correlated or redundant support can still receive credit.

The catch is that a mutation that helps in one state may become impossible to safely reverse in a different counterfactual state. EvoUndo’s move is to split self-modification into representation, synthesis, diagnosis, and independent verification of recoverability. It treats recoverability as something to be checked against alternative states, with an explicit separation between exact state-address grounding and the expressive power of the recovery language, rather than assuming iterative prompting can clean up persistent side effects.

长程 agent 不是算不动，而是工作上下文会越滚越大：历史全留着，检索、整合和决策都被冗余信息拖慢。已有 proactive context management 多靠 search、delete、summarize，工具少，且把不同编辑动作一视同仁，信用分配也只看轨迹末尾回报。

ContextPilot 的动作是把“改上下文”拆成更细的决策：补上 planning、long-term memory 和 soft context offloading 工具，再用 RL 按上下文变化和 entropy 变化挑出关键编辑点做 branch sampling。然后它不把整条轨迹的回报平均摊给每一步，而是从经过同一编辑动作的分支轨迹里估计 action-level advantage，给每个上下文编辑动作单独记账。

摘要只说它在 long-context QA 和 deep search 任务上更强，而且工作上下文更紧凑，并且在不同 base models 和 benchmarks 上都稳定优于已有 baseline；没有给出具体分数。也就是说，收益不只是答得更好，还包括把需要保留的上下文压得更短。

这篇的价值在于，它把“记忆管理”从附属技巧变成了可学习的策略问题，且 credit assignment 比常见轨迹级 RL 更贴近动作本身。限制也很清楚：我这里只看到摘要，没法检查工具增补到底贡献多少、branch sampling 是否稳定、以及紧凑上下文是否在更长任务上仍成立。

Long-horizon agents are not usually bottlenecked by reasoning alone; they bloat their working context until retrieval, integration, and decision-making get dragged down by accumulated history. Prior proactive context management methods mostly rely on search, deletion, and summarization, so they lack global planning, long-term memory, and adaptive compression, and they still treat heterogeneous editing actions as if they had the same effect.

ContextPilot changes the problem from “keep or drop text” into a finer control loop over context edits. It expands the toolset with planning, long-term memory, and soft context offloading, then uses RL to identify critical edits from context and entropy variation, sample branches around those edits, and estimate action-level advantages from all branched trajectories that pass through the same editing decision.

The abstract claims stronger performance on long-context QA and deep search, with a more compact working context, and says this holds across multiple base models and benchmarks. It does not report exact scores, so the main takeaway is directional: the method improves both task outcome and context efficiency, rather than trading one for the other.

The paper’s useful move is to make memory management a first-class policy problem with finer credit assignment than trajectory-level RL. But with only the abstract, it is impossible to tell how much each added tool matters, whether branch sampling is robust, or whether the compactness gains persist on longer or harder settings.

ContextPilot treats proactive context management as a learnable editing policy: the model decides not only what to keep, but also when to plan, when to write to long-term memory, and when to softly offload information from the working context. During training, it first identifies critical editing decisions from context and entropy changes, then samples branches around those decisions and estimates the advantage of each edit from all branched trajectories that pass through the same action, instead of spreading the final trajectory reward uniformly across steps.
